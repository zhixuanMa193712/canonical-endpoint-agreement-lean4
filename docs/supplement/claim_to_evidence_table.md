## Claims not supported

The evidence does not support state-independent tactic substitution; proof-term, dependency, axiom, runtime, or message equality; arbitrary continuation or trajectory equivalence; ecosystem-wide frequency; autonomous theorem-solve-rate improvement; transfer of the multi-step result from `text48_v1` to the strict structural key; training or user-productivity benefit; lossless hard pruning from one-step endpoint equality; or complete-sample 64-root coverage noninferiority in the external hard-root confirmation.
